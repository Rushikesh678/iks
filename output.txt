INPUT:
One day, a girl named Neha went to the market with her mother. She saw a poor child sitting near a shop and asking for food.

SANSKRIT (संस्कृतम्):
एकस्मिन् दिने नेहा नामिका बालिका मातुः सह विपण्यं गतवती। सा एकं दरिद्रं बालकं दुकानस्य समीपे उपविश्य भोजनं याचमानं दृष्टवती।

IAST:
ekasmin dine nehā nāmikā bālikā mātuḥ saha vipaṇyaṁ gatavatī | 
sā ekaṁ daridraṁ bālakaṁ dukānasya samīpe upaviśya bhojanaṁ yācamānaṁ dṛṣṭavatī |

PHONETIC:
ekasmin dine nehaa naamikaa baalikaa maatuh saha vipanyam gatavatee 
saa ekam daridram baalakam dukaanasya sameepe upavishya bhojanam yaacamaanam drishtavatee
